## Proposed Architecture & Design

### 1. Typography & Theme System
- **Fonts**:
  - Urdu Body & Headings: `Noto Nastaliq Urdu` (Google Fonts) with fallback to `Gulzar`, `Jameel Noori Nastaleeq`, system Arabic/Nastaliq.
  - Arabic Verses & Duas: `Amiri` and `Scheherazade New` with high Tashkeel precision and line-heights adjusted (2.0 to 2.4) to eliminate clipping.
  - UI Labels & Numbers: `Outfit` / `Inter` / `Noto Sans Arabic` for crisp counter badges and buttons.
- **Color Palettes**:
  - **Light Theme (Warm Parchment / Mus'haf Paper)**: `#FAF7F0` background, `#2D3748` slate charcoal body, emerald/sage green `#1B5E47` and soft metallic gold `#9B783E` accents, cards `#FFFFFF` with warm border `#EADEC7` and soft elevation.
  - **Dark Theme (Midnight Calm)**: `#0F172A` / `#131B2E` slate night, `#E2E8F0` soft text, `#34D399` sage/emerald highlights, `#F59E0B` warm amber gold, `#1E293B` card backgrounds with subtle borders `#334155`.

### 2. Layout & Sections
- **Sticky App Bar**:
  - Title badge with Islamic geometric accent.
  - Search bar & Section quick-jump tabs (طریقہ کار, قرآنی آیات, مسنون دعائیں, اضافی تدابیر).
  - Controls: Light/Dark Theme toggle, Font size controls (A- / A+), PDF Export button.
- **Hero Section**:
  - Bismillah calligraphic banner, title "الرقیۃ الشرعیۃ — مسنون علاج اور دم کی مکمل گائیڈ", brief explanation of the methodology and spiritual intention.
- **Section 1: طریقہ کار (Methodology & Practical Steps)**:
  - 5 interactive cards: Wudu & Preparation, 7 Sidr Leaves method (Ibn Kathir / Ibn Baz reference), Recitation, Nafth (blowing on water/oil), Usage & Massage.
- **Section 2: قرآنی آیات (Quranic Verses for Ruqyah)**:
  - 8 distinct sections with 12+ individual ayat cards.
  - Arabic text styled in Quranic script with gold highlight framing, Urdu translation in clean Nastaliq, repetition badge, and interactive counter.
- **Section 3: احادیثِ مبارکہ سے مسنون دعائیں (Authentic Prophetic Duas)**:
  - 13 numbered Dua cards with Sahih Bukhari / Muslim / Abu Dawud references.
- **Section 4: اضافی روحانی تدابیر (Additional Spiritual Measures)**:
  - Daily adhkar, Sadaqah, Surah Al-Baqarah recitation at home, and Adhan.
- **Floating Quick Navigation & Progress indicator**.

### 3. PDF Export Implementation
- Integrate `html2pdf.js` via CDN.
- Configured with `html2canvas` (scale: 2 for high-DPI crystal-clear typography) and `jsPDF` (format: A4, orientation: portrait).
- Optimized `@media print` rules:
  - Hides non-printable controls (FABs, header buttons, counters).
  - Uses `break-inside: avoid` / `page-break-inside: avoid` on all cards.
  - Sets clean margin and black/slate typography with high-contrast borders.
